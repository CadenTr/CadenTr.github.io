## Personal assets and project media

`assets/images/nc-topo-qgis-visualization.png` is Caden's digital visualization from the North Carolina layered topographic map QGIS project. It is not a photograph of a completed physical map.

`assets/images/caden-trahan-portrait.jpg` is Caden's local photograph `image0.jpg` and is used with his permission.

`assets/models/nc-topo-layered.glb`, `assets/models/nc-topo-layered.json`, and `assets/images/nc-topo-profile.svg` are web derivatives generated from Caden's cumulative North Carolina QGIS fabrication layers and registration-pin geometry. The underlying elevation source is the [USGS 3D Elevation Program](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program/about-3dep-products-services); the state boundary source is the [U.S. Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/2025/geo/carto-boundary-file.html). The interactive viewer uses the open source [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) library.

`assets/documents/civil-engineering-cad-final.pdf` is Caden's academic Civil Engineering CAD coursework. It is labeled preliminary and not for construction.

`assets/images/paradigm-percussion-logo.png` is the Paradigm Percussion logo from Caden's local program files. The page links to the ensemble's official site for context.

`assets/images/paradigm-performance.webp` and `assets/images/paradigm-2026-boat.webp` are performance images shown on Paradigm Percussion's official [Past Shows page](https://www.paradigmpercussion.com/past-shows), which is linked on the music page as their source.

The music-page audio previews are rendered from Caden's local arrangement files. `assets/audio/pretty-tricky.mp3` was exported locally from `Tricky.mscz`; the MuseScore file and PDF are not linked from the site. Original composer credits are displayed with each project.
